Nutriova – Smart Oil Tracking & AI Healthy Recipe Recommendation App

Nutriova is an Android-based health tracking application developed using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose in Android Studio. The application helps users monitor their daily oil consumption, maintain healthier eating habits, and receive AI-powered healthy recipe recommendations.

The main objective of Nutriova is to spread awareness regarding unhealthy oil intake and encourage users to adopt a balanced and healthy lifestyle through smart meal tracking and personalized recommendations.
